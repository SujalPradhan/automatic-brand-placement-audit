The boards switched to Aramco four seconds before the goal

Spain's winning move was framed by Hisense boards. About four seconds before the ball went in, the LED boards behind the goal changed to Aramco, a top-tier FIFA Partner. Aramco ended with 6 of the 10 brand-seconds we detected around the goal: 60% of goal-moment time, from 12% of all sponsor screen time.

One goal, one board rotation

The goal window runs from the start of the move at 1:03 to the restart at 1:25. Hisense held the boards for the first 3 seconds of the build-up. Then the same LED boards showed Aramco, and stayed on Aramco through the goal and the first replays. Michelob Ultra got one second at the end.

Lenovo had more screen time than any other brand, 16 seconds, and none of it was around the goal.

Aramco's real goal time is likely higher than shown. In the replays from behind the net (1:18–1:21) its logo is large but cut up by the netting, and neither method read it. We checked those frames by eye and left the numbers as measured.
