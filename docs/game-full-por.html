2018 group B · Portugal 3–3 Spain · full match, 1:40

The most-seen board got one second of goal time

In this six-goal draw, Qatar Airways had more sponsor screen time than any other brand (451 seconds, 10.0%) and 1 second inside a goal window. Six goals went to six different sets of boards, led by Adidas, Gazprom, Hyundai, McDonald's, Coca-Cola and Wanda.

Goal by goal

Six goals means six separate windows, so this is a better test than a one-goal clip of whether one brand keeps getting the goals. It didn't. A different brand led every window.

  • Partners kept their share: FIFA's top tier had 61.6% of screen time and 66.1% of goal-moment seconds.
  • Over-index: Hyundai (8.7% to 16.5%), Adidas (8.1% to 14.9%) and Coca-Cola (4.6% to 11.6%).
  • Under-index: Qatar Airways, Visa (9.8% to 2.5%) and Mengniu, which had 253 seconds on screen and none during a goal.
