2022 round of 16 · Brazil 4–1 South Korea · full match, 1:44

Every Brazil goal had a different sponsor behind it

In Brazil's 4–1 win, no brand owned the goals. Each of the five went to a different set of boards: QatarEnergy, Ooredoo and QNB, Budweiser and Hisense, Visa, and Hyundai. The two Qatari brands, Ooredoo and QNB, were 1.3% of all sponsor screen time but 21% of goal-moment seconds, nearly all from one stoppage before the second goal.

Goal by goal

Which brand ended up in the shot depended on where the camera was pointing when the ball went in.

  • Second goal: the camera held on the goal-line boards for about 20 seconds before the goal. Ooredoo and QNB, brands from Qatar that are not in FIFA's sponsor tiers, filled the shot. We checked these frames by eye.
  • Last goal, Korea's: 23 of 24 brand-seconds were Hyundai. Hyundai is a South Korean company, and its boards were in view for almost the whole window.
  • Qatar Airways: 487 seconds on screen, the 4th most of any brand, and 2 seconds during goals.

How well did the tools see this match?

We hand-labelled 30 frames of live play (26 boards) and checked a random 150 detections by eye. Both methods ran on the whole match. Apple Vision took about 22 minutes per hour of video on four workers; Video Intelligence took about 95 minutes of Google processing for the 104-minute match.
