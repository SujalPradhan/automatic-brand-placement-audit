Who owned the goals?

You'd expect the brands paying FIFA the most to own the moments fans replay. In both World Cup finals we measured, they mostly did: FIFA's top-tier partners held 51% of the seconds around the goals in 2022 and 60% in 2026. The exception was a 2026 group game. In Brazil v Morocco, regional supporters, FIFA's lowest tier, held 30% of screen time, and three of them took 72% of goal-moment seconds.

The finals went to the top tier. The group game didn't.

With a third game, a final can be compared with a final. That is the fairer comparison: same stage, and both edits built around the decisive goals. On it, the top tier held on to the goal moments. Our first version of this page compared the 2022 final with the Brazil v Morocco group game and read the drop as a shift. The 2026 final doesn't support that. So far the group game is the outlier.

In every game, the cause is the same: which boards were behind the goal when the ball went in. Both Messi goals in 2022 came in front of Budweiser and Crypto.com, both World Cup Sponsors. In Brazil v Morocco, Vinícius's equaliser was framed for 80 seconds of build-up, celebration and replays by Kraken (a crypto exchange), Betano (a betting brand) and Inter Rapidísimo (a Colombian courier). In the 2026 final, the LED boards behind the goal switched from Hisense to Aramco about four seconds before Spain scored. Each game page shows this goal by goal.

Chinese brands' share of screen time was 24% in the 2022 final (Wanda, Vivo, Hisense, Mengniu), 12% in the group game (Lenovo, Hisense, Mengniu) and 19% in the 2026 final (Lenovo, Hisense). Both 2026 games are below 2022, in line with the drop in Chinese official sponsors from seven in 2018 to three in 2026 (Caixin).

This is three matches, with one highlight edit each. The 2026 final is a 2-minute edit with one goal, and its goal window holds only 10 detected brand-seconds. Which board is lit when a goal goes in is partly luck. Treat these numbers as what to measure across a tournament, not as a trend.

How well can cheap tools see sponsors?

Two free methods, checked by hand

We sample one frame per second. Each frame goes through two methods:

  • On-device OCR that reads board text: the full frame, zoomed tiles for distant boards, and a Chinese-language pass.
  • Google Video Intelligence's logo recognition, keeping only names on the sponsor list.

A brand counts as present in a second if either method finds it.

The review caught errors a raw run would have published. Examples:

  • "UNITED STATES OF AMERICA" read as Bank of America.
  • Aramco's "Global Energy Partner" read as Globant.
  • "AIRWAYS" read as Lay's.
  • The player name "UPAMECANO" read as Betano.
  • "Powered by" on a Lenovo AI graphic read as Powerade.

It also caught one of ours. In the first version of these pages, ADI Predictstreet's boards were counted as Adidas. They are now a separate brand, and the earlier pages' numbers are kept in the benchmark log.

The biggest remaining gap is small, distant boards. We catch about 4 in 10 in the 2022 final, 6 in 10 in the group game, and 1 of 8 in the 2026 final.
