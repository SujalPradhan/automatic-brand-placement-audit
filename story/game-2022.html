2022 final · Argentina 3–3 France (4–2 pens) · Lusail

The banned beer brand got the most goal time

Two days before kickoff, FIFA banned beer sales at Qatar's stadiums (NPR). In the final's highlights, Budweiser still spent more time on screen around the goals than any other brand: 36 seconds. That's 19% of goal-moment time, from 11% of all sponsor screen time.

It came down to two Messi goals

Messi's 23rd-minute penalty and his 108th-minute winner both went in in front of the same stretch of boards: Budweiser and Crypto.com. The winner, with its replays, gave Budweiser 25 seconds and Crypto.com 20. The other goals went to other brands:

  • Di María's goal: Wanda.
  • Mbappé's first penalty: Coca-Cola and Hyundai.
  • Mbappé's volley: Qatar Airways.
  • Mbappé's 118th-minute penalty: Mengniu's Chinese-language boards.
  • The shootout: Visa.

Every sponsor, second by second

Nineteen sponsors appear on the boards. Some never appear during a goal:

  • Hisense and QatarEnergy: 55 seconds between them.
  • The four South American regional supporters: Nubank, Claro, UPL and Inter Rapidísimo.
  • BYJU'S: a large board at 8:30–8:42, then never again.
